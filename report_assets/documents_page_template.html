Sources, methods and limits

Expand counting details

Proposal archive counts come from the published FY2027 DPWH project-document snapshot. Senate counts use the indexed Senate supporting-document links attached to House project rows. Senate and Proposal document records are not the same unit. Project-row presence is a title or code match, not source-content confirmation. Proposal documents attached through a selected NEP counterpart may describe a related scope and should be checked against the House bill.

File matching and parser results are preliminary. Unmatched projects can still have documents outside these snapshots, image-only records may not be recognized, and filenames do not prove document contents.
