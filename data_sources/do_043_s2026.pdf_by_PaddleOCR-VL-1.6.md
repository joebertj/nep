SUBJECT: Interim Guidelines on the Utilization of Construction Material Prices and Equipment Rental Rates Amid Fuel Price Volatility and its Effect to Project Cost Estimates

Recent developments in the global oil market arising from geopolitical tensions between Iran, Israel, and the United States of America have resulted in abrupt increases in fuel and petroleum prices, consequently affecting both the delivered cost of construction materials and the operating cost of construction equipment. In view thereof, the Department recognizes that certain prices reflected in the latest Construction Materials Price Data (CMPD), as well as the Prescribed Equipment Rental Rates (PERR), may no longer reasonably represent prevailing market conditions.

In order to ensure that project cost estimates remain reasonable and reflective of current market conditions, the following interim guidelines are hereby issued.

## 1. Temporary Reference for Construction Material Prices

### 1.1. General Policy

The latest CMPD shall remain the primary reference for construction material prices in the preparation of cost estimates, in accordance with Department Order (D.O.) No. 125, series of 2025, prescribing the Guidelines and Procedures for the Establishment and Utilization of Construction Material Prices, Labor Rates, and Equipment Rental Rates.

However, pending stabilization of market conditions and the issuance of the updated CMPD, Implementing Offices (IOs) may, when warranted, use supplier canvassed prices as a reference for materials which prevailing market prices are no longer adequately reflected in the latest CMPD.

### 1.2. Supplier Canvassing Requirements

The use of supplier canvassed prices shall be supported by:

a. At least three (3) current, independent, and verifiable supplier quotations/canvass for each construction material, reflecting prevailing market prices within or near the project locality; and

b. Clear indication in the canvass documents of the date of canvass, material specifications, unit of measure, value-added tax (VAT) inclusion/exclusion, and applicable delivery conditions.

### 1.3. Reasonable Construction Material Price

a. The adoption of canvassed prices shall be subject to strict comparability requirements, including but not limited to conformity in technical specifications, quality standards,

delivery conditions, and market location. Prices shall, as far as practicable, be sourced from the same locality or, where unavailable, from proximate or economically comparable areas.

b. For purposes of validation, the reasonableness of such canvassed prices shall be benchmarked against authoritative price data, including: (i) the latest available price indices issued by the Philippine Statistics Authority (PSA), particularly the Construction Materials Wholesale Price Index (CMWPI) or any other relevant PSA index applicable to the specific construction material and reference period, and (ii) officially published or maintained price repositories of the Department of Trade and Industry (DTI) (e.g., E-Presyo or other online price monitoring system [OPMS]), whichever is more recent/updated and/or applicable. The selected benchmark shall correspond to the appropriate commodity classification and time period consistent with the date of canvassing.

c. The adoption of a canvassed price for any construction material shall be allowed only when such price is demonstrably consistent with prevailing market movements as reflected in the applicable PSA indices or DTI price data.

Attached to this D.O. as Annex A is the consolidated list of material categories (commodity groups) as defined and published by the PSA on their website, including the corresponding price indices that are regularly updated, and their equivalent materials in the CMPD for each category. Said compilation is hereby prescribed as the official reference guide for: (i) the proper identification and selection of the applicable PSA price index for a given construction material, and (ii) the determination of the permissible price range for purposes of price validation, cost benchmarking, and project cost estimation using the CMWPI. The selection of the appropriate index shall be strictly aligned with the relevant commodity classification and reference period as defined by the PSA.

Likewise, attached to this D.O. as Annex B are the standard computational illustrations covering representative scenarios, demonstrating the comparative application of the CMPD reference price, the canvassed price, and the benchmark price derived from the applicable PSA index. Such illustrations shall serve as the prescribed basis for uniform interpretation and implementation of price validation methodologies across all concerned offices.

## 2. Temporary Reference for Equipment Rental Rates

### 2.1. General Policy

IOs shall continue to adopt the Prescribed Equipment Rental Rates (PERR) in accordance with D.O. No. 125, s. 2025, which is based on the equipment rental rate schedule provided in the Equipment Guidebook issued by the Association of Carriers and Equipment Lessors (ACEL), as duly authorized by the DPWH Secretary.

The prescribed rates shall remain applicable provided that the prevailing fuel price in the project locality does not exceed the maximum fuel price assumption adopted in the prevailing ACEL Equipment Guidebook (now Edition 27). Pursuant to Section 19 (Notes), Item 19.1 thereof, the fuel price considered for operated hour rental rates is P90/liter, considering remote areas and projection for the next five (5) years, in coordination with the DPWH Bureau of Equipment (BOE).

### 2.2. Alternative Equipment Rental Rates

In the event that the prevailing fuel price in the project locality exceeds the ACEL's assumed fuel price in its prevailing equipment guidebook, the IO may determine the appropriate equipment rental rate using the dry rental computation method, in accordance with the formula prescribed by the BOE, taking into consideration the actual prevailing fuel price in the project locality as canvassed, subject however to the conditions and limitations outlined in Sections 1.2 and 1.3 of this policy directive.

The alternative equipment rental rate shall be computed based on the applicable dry rental rate and the corresponding fuel consumption of the equipment unit. The reference table for the corresponding dry rental rates is attached to this D.O. as Annex C and shall serve as the basis for computing the alternative equipment rental rate.

## 3. Documentation and Monitoring

### 3.1. Certification of Compliance

The Heads of IOs shall certify, using the prescribed Certification of Compliance template attached to this D.O. as Annex D, that the construction material prices adopted in the cost estimates are consistent with the benchmark price derived from the applicable PSA index, and DTI price data. The certification shall likewise indicate whether the operated equipment rental rates or dry rental rates were utilized in the preparation of the cost estimates.

The duly accomplished Certification of Compliance shall be attached to, and form an integral part of, the documentary requirements prescribed under the latest Document Tracking System (DoTS) Checklist for the processing and approval of the Program of Works (POW) and the Approved Budget for the Contract (ABC).

### 3.2. Submission Requirements

Certified True Copies (CTCs) of the accomplished Certifications of Compliance for all approved POW/ABC, together with the corresponding canvass and/or price quotation documents used as basis in the preparation of the cost estimates, shall be consolidated by the concerned IO and periodically submitted to the Bureau of Construction (BOC) for monitoring and evaluation purposes.

The specific mode, format, and frequency of submission, together with further clarificatory provisions, shall be prescribed in a separate issuance to be released by the BOC to ensure uniform compliance and proper monitoring by all IOs.

## 4. Application to Projects Under Different Pre-Construction Stages

### 4.1. Projects under Preparation of Cost Estimates

For projects currently under the preparation of cost estimates, the concerned IO shall ensure that the most current, reasonable, and validated price references are adopted in the preparation of POW and ABC, in accordance with the provisions set forth under Sections 1 and 2 of this policy directive.

In view of this, in cases where the adoption of validated construction material prices and/or equipment rental rates in accordance with Sections 1 and 2 of this Department Order results in a computed Project Cost exceeding the authorized funding for the project as provided under the General Appropriations Act (GAA) for Fiscal Year 2026, the concerned Implementing Office (IO) shall undertake the necessary review and rationalization of the project scope, considering the following:

a. The major or principal scope of the project, as defined in the project description/type of work under the Annual Infrastructure Program (AIP) based on the GAA, shall be prioritized and preserved to the fullest extent practicable, together with only those ancillary or supporting components strictly necessary for the functionality, safety, and operational completeness of the project; and

b. The concerned IO shall document the justification and recommend the appropriate course of action, which may include scope restructuring or funding augmentation through appropriate programming or budgetary processes, subject to existing laws, rules, and Department policies.

### 4.2. Projects already Advertised

For projects that have already been advertised for procurement, the bidding process shall proceed in accordance with existing procurement rules and procedures. In case of bidding failure due to an insufficient ABC resulting from updated or prevailing market prices, the concerned IO may undertake the necessary revalidation and reprogramming of the cost estimates using updated and appropriate price references, as in Section 4.1 of these Guidelines, consistent with the provisions of this policy directive and other applicable policies of this Department.

### 4.3. Projects under Contract Perfection

For projects under contract perfection, i.e., with issued Resolution and/or Notice of Award, any increase in construction costs arising from fuel price volatility shall be addressed through price escalation claims during project implementation, in accordance with the applicable provisions of the existing Government Procurement Law and its Implementing Rules and Regulations (IRR), as well as any other relevant guidelines issued by the Department.

## 5. Effectivity Clause

5.1. These Guidelines are issued solely to address temporary market disruptions affecting construction material prices and equipment rental rates due to fuel price volatility. These Guidelines shall remain subject to periodic review and may be revoked upon stabilization of market conditions and the issuance of updated CMPD.

5.2. All IOs are hereby enjoined to exercise due diligence in the canvassing and adoption of prices, ensuring that cost estimates remain reasonable, transparent, and fully consistent with prevailing market conditions.

5.3. This Department Order supplements D.O. No. 125, s. 2025, and D.O. No. 17, s. 2026, and amends certain provisions thereof while remaining consistent with the policies and framework established therein. It shall take effect immediately upon issuance.

## 6. Penalty Clause

Failure to comply with the Department Order may subject the concerned personnel to appropriate administrative action, in accordance with existing laws, rules, and regulations.

# Scenario Computation Sample (Construction Material Price)

To ensure that material prices used in the preparation of cost estimates remain attuned with prevailing market conditions, re-canvassing of CMPD materials suspected to be no longer reflective of current market prices may be undertaken. The median price derived from supplier canvasses may be adopted, provided that it is consistent with the benchmark price derived from the applicable PSA Construction Materials Wholesale Price Index (CMWPI). In all cases, the CMPD price shall serve as the floor price.

Provided on the following sheets is the classification of CMPD materials aligned with the commodity groups of the PSA CMWPI, which shall serve as the reference for determining the applicable CMWPI index in computing the material benchmark price.

### Determination of the Benchmark Price

Compute the Benchmark Price using the formula:

 $$  Benchmark~Price=CMPD~Price~\times~\left(\frac{Latest~CMWPI}{Reference~CMWPI}\right) $$ 

CMPD Price = price of the material reflected in the

CMPD  $ 2^{nd} $ Semester CY 2025

Latest CMWPI = latest available price index of the appropriate commodity group from PSA CMWPI

Reference CMWPI = price index of the appropriate commodity group as of December 2025

## Sample Computation

Material

Material : REINFORCED CONCRETE PIPE, CLASS II (36" | 910 mm Ø)

Commodity Group : Concrete Products

CMPD Price : Php 3,000.00

Latest CMWPI : 125.9 (as of February 2026)

Reference CMWPI : 125.8 (as of December 2025)

 $$  Benchmark~Price=Php~3,000.00~\times~\left(\frac{125.9}{125.8}\right) $$ 

Benchmark Price = Php 3,002.39

In this case, for REINFORCED CONCRETE PIPE, CLASS II (36" | 910 mm Ø), new canvassed prices may be adopted, with Php 3,002.39 as benchmark for the median price.
