HGAB 2027 Project Locator

What the House bill builds in each legislative district

The bill does not sort infrastructure by legislative district. Most of it sits under an agency, often DPWH's Central Office, with the place only in each project's description. This page is our attempt to work out where each project is and add it up for the districts and their representatives. Pick a district for its list of projects, or see what the House changed from the NEP.

By agency/program: choose what to count

The page starts with all five counted. Untick a box to take an agency or program out, or tick it to add it back. The Only this button on a card unticks all the others, so that card is counted alone. The rankings and tables below recount at once.
