# Miagao FY2026 APP, bid results, and quarterly progress review

## Scope and method

This review uses the locally parsed FY2026 Consolidated APP, Q1 bid-result
forms, and Q1 consolidated project/program progress report. Candidate links were
checked against the full APP description, office, scope, quantity, location,
and approved budget. Generic headings and keyword overlap were not treated as
confirmed project matches. Progress amounts are compared only where the report
has a plausible matching title and office; a shared amount alone is not enough.

The source records and original links are retained in
`miagao-official-disclosure-2026.json`. APP row numbers below refer to its
`appLines`; bid and progress row numbers refer to their corresponding parsed
source sheets.

A broader possible match is the Mayor's Office transportation/machinery/roads
program: APP row 372 lists ₱6.8M for fuel, oil and lubricants, while progress
row MO 18 reports the same ₱6.8M under a broader transportation, machinery,
roads/highways, and infrastructure heading. It reports ₱194,567 incurred
(2.86%). Because the progress heading covers more than fuel, this remains a
program-level reconciliation candidate, not a confirmed fuel-only expenditure
match.

## Coverage and follow-up

The Q1 progress extract has 106 rows; 50 contain a nonblank incurred-cost
field and 46 a nonblank completion field. None of the five named bid-result
projects was found by title or barangay in those progress rows. This means the
local records on hand do not connect those bid awards to reported project
delivery; it does not show that work did not occur.

For the three unmatched civil works and the stake truck, check APP amendments,
procurement-monitoring reports, bid abstracts, signed contracts, notices to
proceed, purchase orders, and delivery/completion records. For the five MEO
progress matches, request the missing Q1 expenditure and completion fields or
supporting ledgers. Keep the Q1 Statement of Receipts and Expenditures at its
fund/account level; it cannot fill project-level gaps.

## Source cautions

- The APP is a procurement plan, not an expenditure ledger or proof that a
  contract was awarded as planned.
- Bid-form “winning bidder” fields are not by themselves proof of a signed
  contract, delivery, or completed work.
- Progress rows describe projects/programs and can aggregate multiple
  procurements. Matching totals do not resolve that scope difference.
- Source: official Miagao Consolidated APP, Q1 bid-result workbook, and Q1
  consolidated implementation report, listed with URLs in
  `miagao-official-disclosure-2026.json`.
